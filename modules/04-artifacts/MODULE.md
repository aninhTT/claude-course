# Module 4: Artifacts

Most of your work ends up in front of other people. This module is about making that work **shareable**, and choosing well what's worth sharing. Artifacts are finished pieces of work — docs, decks, designs, dashboards, and short animations — that open beside your conversation, live at their own link, and can be shared, commented on, and edited by your team.

> 💡 **A note for this module:** artifacts work in Claude Code, in chat, and in Cowork. You can do every exercise right here in the course session, or in any Claude conversation.

## What You'll Learn

- What artifacts are, the five types, and when to use an artifact, a Google file, or nothing at all
- How to make **Docs** and **Slides** that start from what your audience needs, and how to cut Claude's draft down until it's ready to share
- How **design systems** keep everything on-brand — and how to use Thumbtack's Thumbprint design with one sentence
- How to build **dashboards** from live data and check where every number comes from
- How to make a short **Motion** animation and **share** artifacts with the right people at the right access level
