# Lesson 4.05: Motion & Sharing

## Learn

This lesson covers two things: the newest artifact type, **Motion**, and how to **share** any artifact with the right people.

> Already comfortable with this topic? Skip ahead anytime with `skip` followed by the next lesson number.

### Motion: Short Animations

**Motion** makes short animations — animated explainers, walkthroughs, animated charts, short demo videos. You describe what you want (and can give it your images, your brand, or screenshots), and Claude animates it. When it's done, you export it as an **MP4** video.

Good uses at work:
- A 30-second explainer of a process people always ask you about
- An animated chart for an all-hands or a team update
- A short walkthrough of a new tool or workflow for onboarding

How Motion works is what makes it easy to edit: Claude builds the animation from code, not by generating video. So you can change one word, one number, or one color **without redoing the rest**. You can scrub through the timeline and **leave comments on a specific moment** ("make this part slower," "this label is wrong") — just like comments in a Doc.

What it *can't* do: Motion doesn't generate realistic footage or people. Think animated graphics, not a filmed video.

> 💡 **Beta:** Motion is in beta and available on Team and Enterprise plans. If you don't see it, your admin may not have turned it on yet. And yes — "Use the Thumbprint design" works here too.

### Sharing: Private Until You Say Otherwise

Every artifact **starts private**. When you're ready, you choose who sees it and what they can do.

**Who you can share with:**
- **Specific people** you invite
- **Everyone at Thumbtack**
- **Anyone with the link** — only if your admin allows sharing outside the company

**What they can do:**
- **View** — read or watch it
- **Comment** — leave feedback (available on some artifact types)
- **Edit** — change it directly

When others have the artifact open, you'll see that they're there. And comments aren't just for people: you can **send a comment to Claude** and have it make the change.

### Sharing Checklist

Before you hit share, run through this:

1. **Does it need to be shared?** Share the one version that fits, in the place your audience already works — an artifact, a Google file, or just a message (Lesson 4.01).
2. **Right audience?** Share with the smallest group that needs it. You can always widen it later.
3. **Right access?** Most people need **view** or **comment**, not **edit**.
4. **Is it ready?** It's written for its audience, as short as it can be, and the ask is easy to find. You can stand behind every part of it (Lesson 4.02). Someone opening the link cold should understand what it is and why they got it.
5. **Anything sensitive in it?** Check for customer data, personal info, or numbers that aren't ready to go out. Our [AI Expectations](https://thumbtack.haystack.so/resources/c8602dcc-0898-4f92-ae93-243ee7d696ba) still apply.
6. **Verified?** If it has facts or numbers, you've checked them (Lesson 4.04).

## Practice

This Practice has two parts. If Motion isn't available to you, do Part B only.

### Part A: Make a Short Motion Piece

**Step 1:** Pick something you explain often — a process, a timeline, a "how this works."

**Step 2:** Ask for a short animation:

> "Make a 30-second Motion explainer of how a request moves through our team's intake process, from submitted to resolved. Use the Thumbprint design."

**Step 3:** Scrub through it. Leave a comment on one moment that needs a change, and have Claude fix it.

**Step 4:** Export it as an MP4.

### Part B: Share an Artifact

**Step 1:** Pick one artifact you made in this module.

**Step 2:** Run through the Sharing Checklist above.

**Step 3:** Share it with one teammate at the access level that makes sense, with a one-line note on what it is and what you'd like from them.

**Success criteria:** Shared at least one artifact with a teammate at an access level you chose on purpose, after running the checklist. If Motion is available to you, also created and exported a short Motion piece.

## Challenge

**Replace a status update with a link.** Think of something you send out regularly — a weekly update, a recap, a metrics email. Rebuild it as an artifact (Doc, Dashboard, or Motion), share it with the people who usually get it, and ask for one round of comments.

Then reflect:
- Was the artifact easier or harder for your audience than what you usually send?
- Did it need to be an artifact, or would a Google Doc or a Slack message have worked just as well?
- Could a skill from Module 3 produce this artifact for you every week?

> **Before you're done — Question It:** What did Claude assume? What's missing? What could break?

**Success criteria:** Replaced one recurring update with a shared artifact, gathered at least one comment, and decided whether a skill could produce it automatically.
