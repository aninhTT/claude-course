# Module 6: Building Your Use Cases

This is the hands-on module. You've planned your use cases — now it's time to build them. Claude isn't just your builder here — it's also your reviewer, pointing out gaps and suggesting improvements.

## What You'll Learn

- How to go from plan to working build
- Getting Claude to review and critique your work
- Iterating and improving based on feedback
- Making builds production-ready and shareable

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Est. Time |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 01 | Build Session: Your Top Use Case | 10-15 min |
| 02 | Claude as Your Reviewer | 5-10 min |
| 03 | Build Session: Your Second Use Case | 10-15 min |
| 04 | Polish & Share | 5-10 min |

## By the End of This Module

You'll have built at least 2 real use cases and learned how to use Claude for continuous improvement of what you build.
